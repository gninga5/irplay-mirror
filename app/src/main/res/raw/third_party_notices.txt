========================================================================================
SONG LYRICS
========================================================================================

While music is playing, iRPlay Mirror looks up the song lyrics online, because AirPlay does
not carry them. Only the song title, artist, album and duration are sent, to these services,
in this order, and only when the TV is connected to the internet: lrcmux.dev (https://lrcmux.dev), LRCLIB (https://lrclib.net) and
lyrics.ovh (https://lyrics.ovh). Lyrics belong to their respective rights holders.
You can turn this off from the home screen ("Song lyrics").
